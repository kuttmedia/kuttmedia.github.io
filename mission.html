Mission

Kutt exists to make video that sounds and looks like the people in it. Clean sound, honest light and an edit that keeps the story moving.

See the work Meet Damo

Why Kutt

Good stories deserve to be heard properly.

A lot of video gets made fast and forgotten faster. Kutt is here for the opposite: podcasts, conference talks, talking heads and promos that people actually watch to the end.

That starts in the studio, with sound and light done right, and finishes in the edit, where every cut has a reason to be there.

What I stand for

Three things every project gets.

  • On set

    Sound first

    People forgive a soft frame. They won't sit through bad audio. Every shoot starts with clean, levelled sound.

  • On camera

    At ease

    Most clients aren't presenters. A calm set and a little direction get you talking like yourself.

  • In the edit

    Kutt with purpose

    The edit is where the story is found. No filler and no gimmicks, just the version that holds attention.
