About

Hi, I'm Damo.

I run Kutt, a one-person video studio. I film, light, record and edit every project myself, from podcasts and conference stages to promos for socials and YouTube.

01 / The short story

Good video feels like a good conversation.

I spend most days in the studio, setting up lights, checking levels and getting people comfortable in front of a lens. The best video doesn't feel produced. It feels like a conversation you didn't want to end.

When the shoot wraps, I edit everything myself, so the person who heard the room is the same person who cuts it. That keeps the story tight and the turnaround quick.

02 / How a project runs

Plan. Shoot. Kutt.

  1. 01

    Plan

    A quick chat about who it's for, where it'll live and what it needs to say. Then I sort the set, lights and schedule.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    A calm shoot day in the studio or on location. Clean sound, flattering light, and plenty of coverage to cut from.

  3. 03

    Kutt

    The edit, colour and sound mix, plus versions sized for YouTube, socials and anywhere else it needs to go.
